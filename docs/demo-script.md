# Five-minute synthetic demo script

Demo with synthetic data. Not for medical use. This is not an emergency service. Prepare a local,
disposable synthetic environment with the necessary patient and physician identities, active
relationship and monitoring consent. Do not use real people, real measurements or patient photos.
Privileged users must be provisioned through the existing trusted process and use MFA. Do not
publish credentials. If an environment is not prepared, describe a step rather than inventing a
result. A recorded walkthrough has not been made.

1. **Context (30 seconds):** show the synthetic-data and non-emergency notice. Explain that no
   clinical validation or clinician approval exists.
2. **Patient entry (60 seconds):** record a synthetic observation and show its source and measured
   time. If demonstrating an alert, enable only the existing unreviewed synthetic-demo fixtures
   and explain that the result is illustrative, not health guidance.
3. **Physician review (60 seconds):** sign in as the authorized synthetic physician, inspect the
   appropriate patient and alert, then show the permitted acknowledge action. Do not show patient
   data to an administrator or an unrelated physician.
4. **Meal photo (60 seconds):** submit a synthetic, non-identifying image and add an authorized
   physician comment. Explain that the feature does not identify foods or estimate nutrients.
5. **Summary and safeguards (90 seconds):** show source-linked deterministic facts, explain that
   AI does not decide alerts, and open the safety case and evaluation report. State that usability
   sessions, clinician/faculty review, and public deployment checks are still pending.
