# Phase 11 human evaluation protocol — pending

Demo with synthetic data. Not for medical use. This protocol is a preparation document, not a
record that participants, sessions, clinician review, or approvals exist. Use synthetic accounts,
synthetic readings, and appropriately licensed or locally generated non-identifying meal images.
Do not collect personal health information or real patient photographs. Record the source and
licence of any external images used. Obtain any institutional permissions required locally.

## Usability sessions

Recruit at least five consenting volunteers, ideally covering patient and physician roles. Explain
that this is a synthetic research prototype, not an emergency service. Give each participant the
same neutral tasks without coaching: submit a synthetic reading, locate its timestamp and any
resulting demo alert, submit a synthetic meal photo, and as the authorized physician locate the
photo and add a non-clinical comment. Ask them to identify the warning and explain what the meal
feature does and does not estimate. Allow keyboard use and record observed accessibility issues.

For each task record completion (completed, assisted, or not completed), elapsed time, notable
errors, and whether the participant understood the synthetic and non-emergency notices. Record
whether help was given; do not infer correctness from completion. Administer the standard
10-item System Usability Scale (SUS) after the tasks, using the established instrument and scoring
method. Record consent and responses separately from identities; report sample size, missing
responses, task denominators, individual or aggregate times as appropriate, and the SUS score
only when enough responses exist. No universal pass threshold is asserted.

## Threshold and wording review

Invite a clinician or faculty reviewer to inspect the existing synthetic-only rule fixtures,
alert wording, non-emergency notice, source timestamps, and review workflow. Ask for dated,
specific feedback and qualifications, whether each concern is unresolved, and any intended
follow-up. Treat comments as feedback, not approval; do not alter thresholds or remove
`requires_clinician_approval` without an authorized decision and a documented review record.
Do not enter real patient data. Record no feedback until it is actually received.
